# Experiment Log

An "experiment" here is any run, build, listening session, bench action or
bounded analysis that answered a stated question with a recorded result:
simulator runs, codec and transport budgets, firmware builds, actions on the
physical radio, and review reproductions. This log replaces the internal work-item
journal; each row keeps only the question, the outcome with its numbers, and
the public evidence. Simulator results describe the model, not radio hardware.
The full review of 2026-10-02 is the authoritative verdict and corrects
several earlier rows. Those corrections are noted inline.

IDs: `MVP-x.y` is the project's internal stage and work-item number (it
matches the `firmware/` directory names, for example `firmware/openrtx-mvp10.3/`);
`E-nnn` are the first simulator experiments; `HW-nn` are actions on the
physical radio; `REV-<date>` rows are reproductions made during a review.

## Verdict legend

| Verdict | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `confirmed` | The hypothesis held. For simulator rows this means "held inside the model". For desk rows it means "stated by a primary source", not measured. |
| `rejected` | The hypothesis failed. A negative result obtained under optimistic assumptions (for example 19.2 kbit/s) still stands, because a real channel would be slower. |
| `inconclusive` | Ran, but the data is too thin, partial, or unpublished to decide. |
| `assumption-based` | The result depends on an unmeasured assumption: mainly the 19.2 kbit/s gross channel rate, 1 ms RX/TX turnaround, fixed 10 ms opportunities or clean links. Not valid on real hardware until G1 is measured. |
| `tooling-only` | A tool, contract, plan or dry run was built. Nothing was measured. |

"Internal record (not published)" means the only detailed record lives in
the project's internal work-item journal or review notes, which are not part
of the public copy. Source files under `internal/` are the code that produced
a simulator result.
